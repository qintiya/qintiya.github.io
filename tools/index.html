ABOUT AWDEV

Useful tools should be easy to reach.

AWDEV is a growing directory of browser-based developer utilities, Islamic resources, and coding education links. Our goal is to make useful resources approachable, fast, and available to everyone.

Many tools on this page run directly in your browser. Avoid entering sensitive information into any online tool unless you understand how it is processed.

Privacy Policy

AWDEV's demo utilities are designed to work in your browser. Some preferences, such as the color theme and tasbih count, are saved in local storage on your device. External links and advertising providers may have their own privacy practices. Do not enter passwords, private keys, or sensitive personal data into public tools.
